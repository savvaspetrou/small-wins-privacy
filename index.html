Privacy Policy

Small Wins: ADHD Tracker

Published by S. Petrou · Last updated September 26, 2026

Our approach

Small Wins is designed to work locally on your device. We do not operate user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking services. We do not sell personal information.

Information you enter

Habit names, schedules, reminders, completion history, notes, values, routines, and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. This information is used only to provide the features you request and is not transmitted to us.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules notifications on your device. Reminder content is not sent to us.

Backups and exports

Automatic cloud backup is disabled. You can create JSON backups and CSV exports using Android’s system file picker. You choose where those files are saved and are responsible for protecting or deleting exported copies.

Sharing and third parties

The app does not transmit habit data to us or share it with third parties. If you choose “Rate the app” or “Send feedback,” Android opens an external app or service. The privacy terms of that external service apply.

Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete habits, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. You may export a copy first from Settings. Because there is no account or server database, we do not retain a separate server-side copy.

Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not specifically directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health information

Small Wins is a self-management and organization tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not a substitute for professional care.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be published on this page and reflected in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact s.petrou@outlook.com.
